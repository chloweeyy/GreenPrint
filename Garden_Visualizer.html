Quick start

How to use the Garden Planner

  1. Open Choose a scene and select a room or outdoor setting.
  2. Drag a plant, pot, or pebbles from Live Inventory onto the plan. Tap an inventory item to place it in the center.
  3. Drop a plant or pebbles onto a pot to combine them. Select a planter to separate, replace, or remove a layer.
  4. Use the view and zoom controls to explore the plan. Drag empty canvas space to pan.
  5. The 2D view is top-down; Angled View adds perspective. The scale marks represent metres.
