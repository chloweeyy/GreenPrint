Quick start

How to use the Garden Planner

  1. Pick a room or outdoor place template.
  2. Drag a plant, pot, or pebbles from Live Inventory onto the plan. Tap an item in the inventory to place it at the center.
  3. Drag a plant or pebbles directly onto a pot to combine them. Select the planter to separate, replace, or remove a layer.
  4. Use the zoom buttons to inspect products. When zoomed in, drag empty space with a finger or mouse to pan around the whole scene. The 2D view is top-down; Angled View adds perspective.
  5. For outdoor plans, loose pebbles can also be placed directly on the ground.
